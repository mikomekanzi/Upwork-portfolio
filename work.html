Landing Page Food & Lifestyle Animation-heavy

Café Street

A coffee shop landing page built around a warm, editorial design language. The brief was simple: make someone feel the smell of fresh coffee through a screen. The entire aesthetic — the floating beans, the circular hero cup, the amber palette — was reverse-engineered from that one sentence.

What mattered most

  • Floating coffee bean parallax on mouse move
  • Product filter tabs with staggered card animations
  • Add-to-cart system with badge bump and toast notifications
  • 3D tilt effect on the hero cup image
  • Scroll-triggered reveal animations throughout
  • Live search filtering across the menu

Landing Page Fitness Dark Theme

FORGE Gym

The challenge here was completely opposite to the café site — instead of warmth, the brief was power, discipline, and edge. The dark cinematic aesthetic with electric green accents was designed to feel like walking into a serious gym at 5AM, not a lifestyle brand.

Focus areas

  • High-contrast hierarchy designed for quick scanning
  • Loader with animated progress bar counting to 100%
  • Hero parallax scroll on background image
  • Fully working BMI calculator with animated SVG gauge needle
  • Pricing toggle with smooth number counter animation
  • Auto-rotating testimonials carousel (5-second interval)
  • Sticky CTA bar appearing after 3 seconds
